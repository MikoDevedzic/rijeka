# Code of conduct

Rijeka is built in public by people who price, hedge, validate and teach for a living, and by people learning
to. Everyone who takes part in it (issues, pull requests, reviews, the validation reports, the messenger of the
hosted demo) is asked to keep to this.

## What is expected

- **Be precise and be kind.** Disagree with numbers, not with people. A finding is stated with the trade, the
  market data and the figure, so that anyone can reproduce it.
- **Credit the work.** Name who found a problem and who fixed it ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- **Assume a newcomer.** A question that looks basic is how someone gets from outside the trading floor to
  inside the mathematics, which is what this project is for.
- **Keep clients out of it.** Nothing from any engagement, employer or counterparty: no names, positions,
  prices, text or code that is not yours to publish.

## What is not accepted

Harassment or insults of any kind, in public or in private; comments about a person rather than their work;
publishing someone's private information; sustained disruption of a discussion; advertising.

## If something goes wrong

Write to **miko@rijeka.app**. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept in confidence, and answered. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block an account from the
repository, and will say why.

This applies to every space of the project and to anyone representing it elsewhere.
