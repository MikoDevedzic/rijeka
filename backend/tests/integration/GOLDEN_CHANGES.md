(1) Initial margin is no longer ISDA SIMM's weight on a sensitivity (the weight table is out of the code): it is an amount the desk sets, or the engine's own estimate, the 99th percentile rise and fall of the set's value over the margin period at each date, about its mean (`pricing/xva_engine.model_im`). For the swap that estimate is about half the SIMM-weighted figure, because it is at the model's current rate volatility (10 days of about 65 bp a year × 2.33 ≈ 30 bp on an IR01 of 4,650) where SIMM's 61 bp weight is calibrated to stress; so MVA is smaller, and with less margin held the exposure left, the replacement cost and the capital are larger. For the forward the old margin ran off in a line to settlement while a forward's exposure does not, which left the later months under-margined: the estimate holds through the life, so the exposure beyond margin, and CVA and FVA with it, nearly vanish. (2) The capital add-on of a currency ticket is SA-CCR's foreign-exchange class (4 % of the notional at spot × the maturity factor, CRE52.72) instead of the interest-rate factor (0.5 % × supervisory duration) the engine had applied to everything: the add-on itself is about twenty times larger (4 % against 0.5 % × a supervisory duration of about 0.41), and with the margin held now smaller than the old weight's and the multiplier nearer one, the forward's KVA is about a hundred times larger in all. (The validator, the same day: the forward's margined CVA of −1.97 is small by construction, the 1 % tail of the model's own move beyond a margin taken at the 99th percentile of that move; it is not evidence that the counterparty risk is that small, and `docs/xva.md` says so.)
