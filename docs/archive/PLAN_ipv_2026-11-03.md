# Plan: independent price verification (P&L → IPV) and the XVA reserves tab

Date: 3 November 2026. Agreed design: the mockup reviewed in chat (inputs by risk ladder, verify by pillar or
surface point, overrides carry their P&L, flash → official signed by the controller, scope filter). Fits the
code as of 0.7.3.

## 0. Principles

1. **The inputs list is derived from the book, never from a catalogue.** `pnl.valuation.trade_curves` and
   `spot_curve` already enumerate what a trade is valued on (curves, `_FUNDING`, `_VOL`, `_SPOT`); the union
   over the trades in scope is the list. The market object additionally records every key it was asked for
   during the run (`Market.reads`), so a read that the ladder cannot bump still appears as a row, flagged.
2. **One sensitivity source.** The pillar ladder is the existing bump-and-reprice through the production
   pricers (`risk/sensitivities.py`), bumping one quote at a time instead of one bucket, with the same valuer
   memo. The surface ladder bumps one surface point at a time: expiry × tenor for swaptions, tenor × (ATM,
   RR25, BF25) for FX, strike × tenor for caps.
3. **First order on the page, full revaluation on apply.** Impact typed = sensitivity × shift. APPLY runs the
   P&L report on a market with the overrides merged (`Market.override`), and the residual is the second-order
   line. The two are never confused.
4. **An override is a market-data event**, with author, reason and both values, never an edit of the desk's
   snapshot. The flash stays the traders' marks.
5. **The gate is explicit.** Official P&L cannot be signed while an input in scope is unsettled or a trade in
   scope could not be valued. Immaterial inputs (sensitivity × tolerance below the firm's threshold) pass as
   WAIVED automatically, labelled.
6. **Module switch.** `IPV` and `XVA_RESERVES` leave "planned" and become switchable; with IPV off the official
   P&L is the flash plus the XVA reserve change; with both off it is the flash.

## 1. Data (`docs/migrations/2026-11-05_ipv.sql`)

- `ipv_tolerances (id, firm_id, input_pattern, pillar_pattern, tol, unit, materiality_usd, note, set_by, set_by_name, created_at)`:
  rules by prefix on the curve id (`USD_SOFR`, `*_VOL`, `*_SPOT`) and the pillar (`*`, `10Y`, `1Yx5Y`); `unit`
  bp (rates), vol_bp (swaption normal vol), pct (spot and FX vol); the first matching rule wins, most specific
  first. Defaults when none: rates 1 bp, swaption vol 1.5 bp, spot 0.10 %, FX vol 0.25 vol points.
- `ipv_runs (id, user_id, run_date, scope jsonb, status OPEN|SIGNED, flash_usd, first_order_usd, second_order_usd,
  xva_usd, official_usd, inputs jsonb, unvalued jsonb, signed_at, signed_by, signed_name, created_at)`: one row per
  account, date and scope; `inputs` holds each input's row (pillars or points, desk and independent quotes,
  sensitivities, state, who and when).
- `ipv_overrides (id, user_id, run_date, curve_id, pillar, desk_value, override_value, reason, author_user_id,
  author_name, created_at, withdrawn_at)`: the market-data events.
- `ipv_independent` is not a table: an independent mark is a `market_data_snapshots` row with `source =
  'IPV_INDEPENDENT'` (uploaded by a controller through `POST /api/ipv/independent`, or written by a self-host's
  second feed). On the hosted demo there is none unless uploaded, and the page says "no independent source:
  model IPV only" for that input.
- `pnl_signoffs` gains the role `IPV` for the official number; no schema change.

## 2. Engine (`backend/ipv/`)

- `inputs.py`: `dependencies(trades) -> {curve_id: [trade ids]}` from `trade_curves` + `spot_curve`;
  `kind_of(curve_id)` → RATES | FUNDING | SWAPTION_VOL | FX_VOL | CAP_VOL | SPOT | CDS; `pillars_of(curve_id,
  quotes)` → the pillar keys (tenors; `AxB` surface points; `tenor|ATM/RR25/BF25` for FX).
- `ladder.py`: `pillar_ladder(db, user, trades, d, mkt, curve_id) -> {pillar: sensitivity_usd}`: bump one quote
  (1 bp, 1 vol bp, 1 % spot), reprice the trades in scope, sum in USD; surfaces by point. Cost: pillars ×
  trades valuations, memoised; a 1,000-trade book on twelve SOFR pillars is twelve book revaluations, the same
  order as the nightly VaR.
- `tolerances.py`: `rule_for(rules, curve_id, pillar)`, `status(desk, independent, tol, override) -> OK |
  REVIEW | OVERRIDDEN`, `material(sensitivity, tol, threshold)`.
- `run.py`: `build(db, user, d, scope)` → the run: trades in scope (desk / book / trader from the trade's
  columns and the org nodes), dependencies, for each input the desk quotes (the snapshot the book was priced
  on), the independent quotes (`IPV_INDEPENDENT` at or before `d`), the pillar ladder, the rule, the state
  (carried from the stored run when it exists), the flash (the trading P&L's day-on-day at the scope, from
  `pnl.report` with `prefer_stored`), the XVA reserve change (`pnl.xva.section`), the overrides and their
  first-order impact; `apply(db, user, run)` → the P&L report on the overridden market, the official rows, the
  second order; `gate(run)` → the list of what blocks the sign-off; `sign(db, user, run)` → status SIGNED, the
  `pnl_signoffs` row with role IPV and `usd_signed = official`.
- `store.py`: read and write `ipv_runs`, the overrides, the approvals.

## 3. Routes (`api/routes/ipv.py`, `/api/ipv`)

- `GET /run?date=&scope=` the stored run (or `live=1`); `POST /run` enqueues `ipv_run` on the worker (a thread
  without one), `GET /run/status`.
- `PUT /override` `{date, scope, curve_id, pillar, value, reason}`; `DELETE /override/{id}` withdraws.
- `POST /inputs/approve` `{date, scope, curve_id}` and `/inputs/waive` `{…, reason}`; `POST /apply` (the full
  revaluation); `POST /signoff` `{date, scope, comment}` (controller or management; the gate must be empty).
- `GET/POST/DELETE /tolerances` (risk or controller writes), `POST /independent` `{curve_id, date, quotes}`.
- `GET /api/pnl/report` gains `official` (the signed run's figures) beside the existing totals when a run exists
  for the date, so the trading P&L shows FLASH and OFFICIAL.

## 4. Frontend

- `pnl/PnlNav.jsx`: TRADING P&L | XVA RESERVES | IPV, modules respected.
- `pnl/IpvPage.jsx` + `ipv-page.js` (pure, tested): the mockup as built: scope, cards, the inputs table, the
  pillar panel and the surface grid, the waterfall, APPLY / APPROVE / WAIVE / SIGN OFF, the audit trail.
- `pnl/XvaReservesPage.jsx`: the reserve levels per counterparty and component from the credit engine's stored
  run, day on day, accounting (netted) against economic (memo) per the firm's policy, the sign-off state. The
  sequential explain of the reserve change is the second step of this tab.
- `configurations/IpvTolerances.jsx` under P&L & CONTROL.
- `PnlPage.jsx`: FLASH and OFFICIAL chips and the official total when a signed run exists.

## 5. Tests

- Unit: dependencies of a mixed book; pillar keys per kind; the tolerance matcher and the materiality rule; the
  state machine and the gate; first order = Σ sensitivity × shift; second order = official − flash − first
  order on a stubbed report; the sign-off writes the IPV role.
- Integration on the stack: a run on the seeded book (live), an override on a SOFR pillar with its first-order
  impact, APPLY with a non-zero second order, approvals, a blocked sign-off (one input unsettled), the sign-off,
  the P&L report's `official`; the tolerance routes; an independent upload and the REVIEW status it produces.
- The existing P&L and risk suites unchanged.
