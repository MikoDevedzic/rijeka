# Model inventory

Every model in the engine, where it lives, where the methodology describes it, how it is checked and
what is open. Reference rows are the QuantLib comparisons in `backend/tests/reference/` that run on
every commit (436 in all; a row fails the build when its difference exceeds its tolerance). Open
findings carry the `validation` label on GitHub. Updated 16 November 2026 (a collar at the ticket's frequency; the scenario of an option of any style, a Bermudan's σ under a scenario), 15 November 2026 (the scenario shifts of a rates ticket), 15 November 2026 (the ladder at each quoted tenor) and 9 November 2026 (the model validator's first run: the venue surface row, and what the ladder, the NDF and the vol history rows did not say).
